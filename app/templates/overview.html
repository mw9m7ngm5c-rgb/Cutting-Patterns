Workflow

  1. Logs: define log classes, then type, paste or generate logs.
  2. Products: sizes (dry and wet), which products the mill sells and their prices, wane rules.
  3. Machines: kerfs, resaws and the edger for each production line.
  4. Sawing patterns: build patterns for each class and try them on single logs.
  5. Batch runs: saw every pattern on its class and keep the results.
  6. Reports: one-liner, board and summary reports, printable or as Excel.
