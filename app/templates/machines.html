{% elif tab == "secondary" %}

The cant is turned and sawn into a stack of boards, centred on the saw, one kerf apart. Cant guiding saws round the sweep: half taper follows the log's centreline, full taper follows the top face of the cant so the taper all falls on the other side. Sweep beyond the largest the saw can follow is sawn straight. Cant misalignment skews the cant end to end; the secondary saw offset moves the whole stack up.
