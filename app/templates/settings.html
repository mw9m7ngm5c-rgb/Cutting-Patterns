Settings

Log volume

Log volume = π/4 × (D + ½ × L × taper)² × L. Each of D, L and taper can be the log's actual value or a nominal one.

Log model

Sawing

Arris alignment applies to secondary patterns with a comma, such as 25 3*38,25: the stack is moved so the blade at the comma sits where the cant's sawn faces run out of wood.

Real-log variation

Ideal logs are smooth: straight taper, a perfect ellipse, a single even sweep. Real logs are not. Switch this on to saw every log with random irregularities of the sizes below. Each log keeps the same shape every time it is sawn (drawn from the seed and its log number), so runs stay comparable. Used by batch runs, the sawing-pattern screen and the generator.

The typical ranges are a starting point, not measured at Ngome; see docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md A-55.
