Our family has been part of the South African timber industry for more than 80 years. Since the 1940s we have run sawmills and pallet manufacturing operations, learning the trade log by log and passing that knowledge from one generation to the next.

That experience, in how to grade a log, how to set up a saw line and how to keep customers coming back year after year, is now behind Thorpe Timbers.

Today the fourth generation of the family carries that work forward in Ngome.
